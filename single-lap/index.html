Scope and Input Limits

The model evaluates the WAAM austenitic or duplex stainless steel plate in a non-preloaded, single-bolt, single-lap connection loaded in tension. The round hole is centred across the plate width. The companion plate is assumed to remain intact. Bolt failure, companion plate failure and frictional slip are outside the plate resistance model.

Use positive dimensions and measured or specified plate strengths in the direction of loading, at angle θ to the printing direction. The nominal bolt diameter is a whole number in millimetres. The hole diameter may include decimals and must satisfy the calculator's standard-hole clearance check.

For the proposed methods, use e1/dh ≥ 1.2, e2/dh ≥ 1.2 and t ≤ 5 mm as recommended limits. The parametric study considered thicknesses of 3 and 5 mm. Values outside the recommended limits are extrapolations.

Governing Plate Resistance

Each standard and proposed method reports the smallest of the net-section rupture (NSR), shear-out or end-distance-controlled (SOF), and bearing (BF) resistances. In the Eurocode comparisons, SOF identifies the end-distance branch of the bearing equation. BF identifies the bearing cap.

Bolt tilting and end curling may accompany failure in single shear. The reported governing modes identify the controlling resistance branches. Guo et al. provide a shear-out reference using p = 0.10. Teh & Uz provide a tilt-bearing reference based on the net width b − dh for comparison with localised tearing (LTF) and curl-bearing failure (CBF) in the study. Each literature value represents its own branch.

Design Methods

ANSI/AISC 360-22
Provisions for structural steel buildings. The comparison uses the net shear plane and a bearing coefficient of 3.0.
ANSI/AISC 370-25
Provisions for structural stainless steel buildings. The comparison uses the gross shear plane and a bearing coefficient of 2.5 when e2/dh > 1.5, or 2.0 otherwise.
ASCE/SEI 8-22
Provisions for cold-formed stainless steel structural members. The single-shear bearing coefficient is 2.65 × 0.75 for the no-washer configuration. Results for t > 4.7625 mm are shown for comparison outside the thickness scope used in the study.
EN 1993-1-8:2024
Provisions for structural steel joints. The study's single-lap comparison uses a gross shear plane, km = 1.0 for fy ≤ 460 MPa and 0.9 otherwise, and a bearing cap of 1.5dbtfu.
EN 1993-1-4:2025
Supplementary provisions for stainless steel structures. The thin single-lap branch applies at t ≤ 4 mm with a bearing coefficient of 1.6. For t > 4 mm the shear and bearing branches depend on e2/dh.
Proposed-nv and Proposed-gv
Both methods use Cn,pro = 0.85 + 0.15dh/b for net-section rupture and a bearing cap of 2.2dbtfu. Proposed-nv retains the AISC 360 net shear-plane expression. Proposed-gv uses a gross shear-plane coefficient of 1.1 on (e1/dh)dbtfu.
