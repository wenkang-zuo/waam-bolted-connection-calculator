Scope and Input Limits

The model evaluates the inner WAAM austenitic or duplex stainless steel plate in a non-preloaded, single-bolt, double-shear bearing-type connection with a standard circular hole. Bolt failure, frictional slip, bolt pretension and outer cover plate failure are outside the scope.

Use positive dimensions and plate strengths in the loading direction, with fy ≤ fu. Use a whole-number db up to 30 mm. The hole diameter may include decimals and must satisfy the calculator's standard-hole clearance check. Both distances must exceed dh/2 and b = 2e2.

For the proposed methods, use e1/dh ≥ 1.2, e2/dh ≥ 1.2 and t ≤ 5 mm as recommended limits. Results outside these conditions are extrapolations.

Governing Plate Resistance

Each method reports the smallest of the net-section rupture (NSR), shear-out or end-distance-controlled (SOF), and bearing (BF) resistances.

Design Methods

ANSI/AISC 360-22
Provisions for structural steel buildings. The comparison uses net-section rupture, the net shear plane and a bearing cap.
ANSI/AISC 370-25
Provisions for structural stainless steel buildings. The comparison uses net-section rupture, the gross shear plane and a bearing cap.
ASCE/SEI 8-22
Provisions for cold-formed stainless steel members. The comparison uses modified net-section rupture, the net shear plane and the double-shear bearing cap.
EN 1993-1-8:2024
Provisions for steel joints. The comparison uses net-section rupture and decomposes the bearing resistance into an end-distance branch and a bearing cap.
EN 1993-1-4:2025
Provisions for stainless steel structures. The comparison uses net-section rupture and edge-distance-dependent shear-out and bearing branches.
Literature
The comparison uses the shear-out expression of Guo et al. and the bearing cap of Teh and Uz.
Proposed-gv
Recalibrated gross shear-plane method for WAAM austenitic and duplex stainless steel.
Proposed-av
Recalibrated active shear-plane method for WAAM austenitic and duplex stainless steel.
